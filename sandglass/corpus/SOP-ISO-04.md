# Isolation and Lockout-Tagout

## 1 Isolation standard

Hydrocarbon service above 10 barg requires double block and bleed isolation. Lower-pressure service requires at least a single locked-closed valve plus a blind or spade.

## 2 Verification

Each isolation must be verified by a second competent person, who checks zero energy (no pressure, no flow, no voltage) before work starts.

## 3 Handover

Isolations that remain in place at shift change are handed over line by line from the isolation register. The incoming operator reads back each tag and state. An isolation that cannot be verified physically must be treated as not in place.
