# Gas Detector Bypass and Inhibit

## 1 Authorisation

A fixed gas detector may only be bypassed or inhibited with the written approval of the shift supervisor, recorded in the bypass log with the tag, reason and expected return time.

A bypass may last at most 8 hours. Any bypass that must continue beyond 8 hours requires re-approval by the operations superintendent.

## 2 Compensating measures

While a fixed gas detector is bypassed, a portable gas monitor with alarm must be placed at the detector location and checked every hour by the field operator.

Hot work within 15 metres of a bypassed detector is suspended. It may resume only when the detector is back in service, or when the area authority confirms a continuous portable monitor and a dedicated gas watcher are in place.

## 3 Handover

Every active bypass must be stated at shift handover, including the tag, the time it was applied and the compensating measures in place. The incoming supervisor must read back each active bypass.

## 4 Detector coverage, Unit 3

GD-311 and GD-312 cover the stripper platform in area 3C. GD-301 covers the feed pump bay in area 3B.
