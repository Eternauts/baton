# Operations Bulletin 2026-07: Stripper Platform Hot Work

Issued 2026-09-20 by the operations superintendent. This bulletin supersedes SOP-GD-03 section 2 for the stripper platform (area 3C) only, until withdrawn.

## Requirement

On the stripper platform, hot work may not resume while GD-311 or GD-312 is bypassed, even with a portable monitor and a gas watcher in place. The detector must be returned to service and tested first.

## Reason

Two gas releases on the platform in 2026 were not detected by portable monitors because of wind direction.
