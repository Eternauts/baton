# Hot Work Permit Procedure

Kestrel Bay Demo Plant (fictional). Applies to all welding, grinding, cutting and any open flame or spark-producing work in process units.

## 1 Scope and authority

A hot work permit is issued by the area authority (shift supervisor) and is valid for one shift, at most 12 hours. It must be revalidated at every shift handover by the incoming shift supervisor before work resumes.

## 2 Pre-start conditions

Before hot work starts, all process equipment within 15 metres of the work point must be isolated, drained and free of hydrocarbons, or physically shielded with fire blankets.

No flange, manway or line within 15 metres of the work point may be open to atmosphere while hot work is in progress. If a line is broken during the permit, hot work must stop until the opening is blinded or closed.

## 3 Gas testing

A gas test with a calibrated portable four-gas monitor is required before work starts and at least every 2 hours during work. Hot work must not start or continue if the flammable gas reading exceeds 10% LEL.

## 4 Fixed gas detection

Fixed gas detectors covering the work area must be in service. If any fixed gas detector covering the work area is bypassed, inhibited or faulty, hot work must stop immediately and may only resume under the compensating measures in SOP-GD-03.

## 5 Fire watch

A trained fire watch with a charged hose or extinguisher must be present for the whole job and for 30 minutes after hot work ends.
