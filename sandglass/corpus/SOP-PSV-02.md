# Relief Valve Out of Service

## 1 Principle

Equipment must never be operated without overpressure protection. When a pressure safety valve (PSV) is removed for testing or repair, the equipment it protects must stay isolated and depressurised until the PSV is reinstalled and its isolation valves are locked open.

## 2 Removal

The PSV inlet and outlet must be isolated with locked-closed valves and the open flanges fitted with blind flanges before the valve is removed. The removal is recorded in the isolation register.

## 3 Protected equipment

The protected equipment must be tagged "DO NOT START - RELIEF REMOVED" at the local start station and in the control system. Starting protected equipment while its PSV is removed is prohibited, including for short tests.

## 4 Reinstatement

After the bench test, the PSV is reinstalled with new gaskets, the isolation valves are locked open, and the shift supervisor signs the reinstatement in the isolation register before the start prohibition is lifted.
