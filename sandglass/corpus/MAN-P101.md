# Feed Pumps P-101A/B Operating Manual

## 1 Equipment

P-101A and P-101B are the Unit 3 feed pumps in area 3B, one running and one on standby. PSV-104 protects the P-101A discharge and PSV-105 protects the P-101B discharge. Both relief valves are set at 18 barg.

## 2 Switching pumps

To switch duty, start the standby pump against a closed discharge valve, open the discharge slowly, confirm flow, then stop the running pump. Never switch to a pump whose relief valve is out of service.

## 3 Trips and alarms

Each pump trips on low suction pressure below 1.2 barg and on high motor winding temperature. A high-vibration alarm at 7 mm/s requires the field operator to check the pump within 30 minutes.
