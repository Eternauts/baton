# Emergency Shutdown Valve Bypass and High Level Protection

Kestrel Bay Demo Plant (fictional). Applies to emergency shutdown valves (ESDV) on Unit 01, including ESDV-301 on the inlet of storage tank TK-501.

## 1 Authorisation

An ESDV bypass or override may only be applied with the written approval of the shift supervisor, recorded in the bypass log with the tag, the reason and the expected return time. A bypass may last at most 4 hours. Longer bypasses need the operations superintendent.

## 2 Compensating measures

While ESDV-301 is bypassed, the tank level of TK-501 must be read manually every 30 minutes, the high-level alarm must stay in service, and no transfer into TK-501 may start without a supervisor check.

## 3 High level limits

The TK-501 high level alarm is set at 85 percent and the high-high trip at 92 percent. If the level is above 80 percent and rising while ESDV-301 is bypassed, transfers into TK-501 must stop.

## 4 Handover

Every active ESDV bypass must be stated at shift handover with the tag, the time applied and the compensating measures. The incoming supervisor must read back each bypass. A bypass found in the control system but not mentioned in the handover is treated as an unflagged safeguard bypass and escalated to the supervisor immediately.
