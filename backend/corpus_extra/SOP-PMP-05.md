# Pump Seal Replacement and Pump Isolation

Kestrel Bay Demo Plant (fictional). Applies to the Unit 01 hydrocracker charge pumps P-201A and P-201B. Both pumps take suction from drum V-102.

## 1 When a seal must be replaced

A mechanical seal that is visibly wet, or a pump with vibration above 7 mm/s, must be reported at handover and scheduled for repair. A pump with a wet seal and rising vibration must not be left running unattended. Move duty to the standby pump before starting seal work.

## 2 Isolation before seal work

The pump must be fully isolated before the seal is touched: stop the pump, close and lock the suction and discharge block valves, apply double block and bleed, then drain and depressurise the casing. Isolation must follow SOP-ISO-04. Each lock is tagged with the permit number.

## 3 Verification

A second competent person verifies zero energy: no pressure on the casing gauge, no flow, motor breaker racked out and locked. A pump showing a physical state of OPEN or RUNNING in the control system is not isolated, whatever the log says, and no seal work may start.

## 4 Return to service

After the seal is replaced, remove locks in reverse order against the isolation register, fill and vent the casing, and start the pump against a closed discharge valve. The shift supervisor signs the return to service. Any isolation still in place at shift change is handed over line by line under SOP-ISO-04 section 3.
